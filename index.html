FAQs

Before You Call, Check Here

If your question is not here, it is still worth a quick call.

Are your plumbers licensed and insured in Oakville?

Yes. Oakville Plumbing Pros works under a valid plumbing licence with full insurance, and anything requiring a permit is filed with the Town of Oakville and inspected under the Ontario Building Code before we call it done.

Can you get here fast for a plumbing emergency?

We keep emergency slots open daily for Oakville and the close-in Halton area. Burst supply lines, sewer backups, and no-water calls get priority routing, and common repair parts are already on the truck.

Our tank just started leaking. How quickly can it be replaced?

Often the same visit. We stock the common residential sizes and can execute a tank or tankless swap across Oakville within hours of the call, including the haul-away and the permit filing.

Is it worth repiping an older Oakville home before renovating?

Almost always. Opening walls twice is the expensive way to do it. If your Old Oakville or Kerr Street home still has galvanized or polybutylene supply lines, replacing them during the renovation saves real money and grief.

Do you work on homes with wells around Oakville?

Yes. Properties along the escarpment toward Campbellville and Nassagaweya often run private wells with pressure tanks, iron filters, and UV systems. We service and install all of it, along with the septic-side plumbing.

Does Oakville water really need a softener?

Lake Ontario supply here is moderately hard, enough to spot glassware and scale shower glass over time, and rural wells nearby run much harder. A test tells us quickly whether treatment pays off in your home.

How do you handle pricing and quotes?

Quotes are free and written, and the number we give is the number you pay. If work is optional rather than necessary, we say so plainly, and nothing proceeds without your approval.

Which communities do you serve around Oakville?

Oakville first, plus Burlington, Mississauga, Milton, Georgetown, and Campbellville. If you are close by and unsure, call and ask, because we would rather give you a straight answer than a maybe.
